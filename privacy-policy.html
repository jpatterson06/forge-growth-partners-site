Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AllStar BJJ LLC, doing business as Forge Growth Partners ("Forge Growth Partners," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit forgegrowthpartners.io or interact with our forms, quizzes, calls, or text messages.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Gym or business details (name, size, revenue range, current software tools, location)
  • Information submitted through our contact form or AI Gym Health Score™ quiz, including your responses about your business and its challenges
  • Messages, notes, or other content you send us directly

We also automatically collect limited technical information when you visit our site, such as your browser type, referring page, and general marketing attribution data (for example, which ad or search term brought you to the site), used to understand how visitors find us.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and follow up on requests for a consultation or strategy call
  • Schedule, confirm, and send reminders for appointments you book with us
  • Generate and deliver your AI Gym Health Score™ results
  • Send you calls or text messages about your inquiry, where you have given consent to do so (see "SMS and Calling Consent" below)
  • Improve our services and understand how visitors use our site

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your phone number or SMS consent with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

SMS and Calling Consent

If you provide a phone number through our contact form or AI Gym Health Score™ quiz, you will be asked to check a box confirming your consent to receive calls and text messages from Forge Growth Partners, including by automated technology, about your inquiry. This consent is not a condition of receiving any service or product — it is optional, and the box is not checked by default.

We log the exact consent language shown to you and the time you agreed to it, so we can maintain a record of your opt-in.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message we send. Reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your interactions with us.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use third-party providers to operate our business, including Twilio (for calls and text messages), Supabase (for secure data storage), Netlify (for website hosting), and Cal.com (for appointment scheduling). These providers process your information only as necessary to provide their services to us and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, maintain our business records, and comply with legal obligations, including maintaining a record of SMS consent.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw consent to be contacted, at any time by emailing us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

Our services are directed at business owners and are not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at jpatterson@allstarbjj.com.
