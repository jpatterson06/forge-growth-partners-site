Jamal Patterson has been on both sides of the equation — competing at the highest levels of the sport and building a martial arts business from the ground up in a competitive market.

After founding AllStar Martial Arts in Union, NJ in 2011, Jamal spent years figuring out what actually works — not what gym consultants who've never run a dojo think works. The systems he built there became the foundation for Forge Growth Partners — and eventually, JFIVE, our proprietary AI practice management system.

Over the years, those same systems — curriculum frameworks, AI automation, retention infrastructure — have been applied to gyms across the country. Forge Growth Partners is the formalization of that work, with a specialized team assembled to deliver it at scale.
