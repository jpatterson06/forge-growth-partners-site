From Goiânia to Miami

A Brazilian Academy, Built by a Brazilian Fighter

Andre Machado Gusmão was born in Goiânia, in the state of Goiás, Brazil. Before he ever put on a gi, he was playing capoeira. It was his first martial art, and it is still the heartbeat of this academy. Capoeira is where Brazilian music, dance, and fighting meet, and Andre grew up inside that culture, in the roda, with the berimbau setting the rhythm.

In 2001 he moved to New York and started teaching capoeira, becoming the highest-ranking representative of Grupo Capoeira Nagô in the United States. Jiu-jitsu came next. As a blue belt he reached the final of the 2000 IBJJF World Championship, and in New York he trained under Renzo Gracie, earning his black belt in one of the most respected lineages in Brazilian jiu-jitsu.

Then came the cage. Andre went 5-0 to start his professional MMA career, helped the New York Pitbulls win the 2007 IFL World Team Championship, and made his UFC debut at UFC 87 in 2008 against a fellow debutant named Jon Jones. Capoeira was his base the whole way. Commentators called his style unusual. His students call it fun.

In the summer of 2016, Mestre Andre opened Andre Gusmão Academy in Miami to bring all of that home: the discipline of jiu-jitsu, the conditioning of Muay Thai, and the joy of capoeira, taught the way it is taught in Brazil, to families in Miami.

Community

Serving Miami the Brazilian Way

Miami is home to one of the largest Brazilian communities in the United States, and this academy is a piece of Brazil in the middle of it. Portuguese and English are spoken on the mats. Kids learn capoeira songs the way kids in Goiânia learn them. Families from Little Haiti to Brickell train together, and after class nobody is in a hurry to leave.

The academy hosts rodas, belt and cord ceremonies, seminars with visiting mestres and black belts, and competition teams that represent Miami at IBJJF events. If you are Brazilian and missing home, or you have never been to Brazil and want to understand why the culture is so loved, come to a roda.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience to start jiu-jitsu or Muay Thai?

No. Most members start with zero experience. Beginner classes teach the basics step by step, and your training partners will help you, not hurt you.

What is capoeira, and is it good for kids?

Capoeira is a Brazilian martial art that combines kicks, dodges, acrobatics, music, and song, practiced in a circle called the roda. Kids love it because it is playful and musical, and parents love it because it builds coordination, rhythm, confidence, and respect. Our kids capoeira classes are taught by Capoeira Nagô instructors.

How much does it cost?

Your first class is free. New members can get 4 weeks of unlimited training for $49. After that, monthly memberships depend on the program and how many days a week you train. Call (305) 409-0991 for current pricing.

What should I wear to my first class?

For jiu-jitsu or capoeira, comfortable athletic clothes with no zippers or pockets. For Muay Thai, shorts and a t-shirt. We have loaner gis and gloves for your trial class.

Where do you get your black belts from?

Mestre Andre is a second-degree black belt under Renzo Gracie. Professor Thiago Pereira is a black belt under Jeferson Moura in the Gracie Barra lineage. Our capoeira instructors are graded through Grupo Capoeira Nagô, founded by Mestre Pequinês in Goiânia, Brazil.

Do you have women's classes and self-defense?

Women train in all of our programs, and jiu-jitsu in particular is built for smaller people to control larger ones. Ask about women's self-defense seminars when you book your trial.
